# Xiaodou look mechanics

The shoes, skirt and lower torso stay anchored on the idle ground baseline. The neck pivots the large head toward the screen-space target. The head pitches visibly up or down and yaws modestly left or right, while the eyes rotate coherently within their original sclera, iris, pupil and eyelid construction. The nose tip and chin visibly follow the direction; the bob remains attached and follows the head. Small upper-torso follow-through is allowed, with legs and feet planted.

Up: head tipped back, chin raised, nose tip and pupils aim upward, lower face foreshortened. Down: chin tucked, nose and pupils aim down, more hair crown visible. Right: face and nose yaw toward screen-right, right cheek foreshortened and pupils aim screen-right. Left is the corresponding physical head turn toward screen-left. Keep enough face visible throughout to preserve identity.

Diagonals combine pitch and yaw in even steps, with no whole-character turntable, sprite rotation, replacement eyes or isolated pupil-only motion. Head, hair and eyes move as a physically coherent family. Clothes, material, lighting, hair part, button count, skirt and proportions match the approved initial concept.
